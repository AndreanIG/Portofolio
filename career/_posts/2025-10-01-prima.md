<section class="lead" style="text-align: justify;" markdown="1">
In October 2025, I joined <strong>Prima PMI</strong> in Melbourne as a <strong>Machine Operator (Christmas Casual)</strong> in the Calender Department. It was my first experience working in a full-scale production facility, where I supported high-volume manufacturing during the busiest season of the year. This role taught me adaptability, discipline, and the importance of working efficiently as part of a larger operational system.
</section>

---

## 🏭 Working in the Calender Department

The Calender Department is responsible for handling materials that go through pressing, rolling, or smoothing processes using heavy industrial machinery.  
As a machine operator assistant, my daily tasks revolved around supporting this workflow:

- Operating and assisting multiple machines during peak production  
- Performing routine maintenance checks such as cleaning, lubrication, and setup  
- Troubleshooting small issues to minimise downtime  
- Monitoring material quality and ensuring consistency  
- Handling RF scanning for inventory and work-tracking  
- Supporting heavy lifting, material movement, and loading tasks  

Working with different machines required quick learning and constant awareness.  
Every shift was fast-paced, but it sharpened my ability to adapt across roles and maintain focus under physical and time-based pressure.

---

## 📦 Learning Production Workflow

One of the most valuable aspects of the role was understanding how each step in a manufacturing line connects to the next.  
Even a small delay in one stage could impact the entire production chain.  

I learned to:

- Communicate clearly with supervisors and teammates  
- Prepare materials ahead of schedule to prevent bottlenecks  
- Prioritize tasks during high-volume processing  
- Identify defects or irregularities before they affected output  

The experience helped me appreciate the importance of precision and timing in industrial operations—skills that bridge both technical and non-technical fields.

---

## 🤝 Teamwork in a Fast-Paced Environment

Working as a Christmas Casual meant joining a fully functioning team during their busiest period.  
I quickly learned how essential teamwork is on a production floor.  

Every shift required coordination:

- Passing materials between operators  
- Sharing machine responsibilities when workloads spiked  
- Supporting teammates during maintenance or slowdowns  
- Keeping communication open to avoid safety risks  

The team at Prima PMI was supportive, patient, and collaborative.  
They taught me not just the technical tasks but also the discipline and mindset needed to work efficiently in a manufacturing environment.

---

## 🧠 Skills I Developed

Although the role was outside my usual background in IT and data science, it helped me build skills that are valuable across industries:

- Adaptability under pressure  
- Operational discipline  
- Basic mechanical understanding  
- Quality control mindset  
- RF scanning and inventory handling  
- Physical endurance and workflow awareness  
- Communication and coordination in shift-based teams  

These experiences strengthened my readiness for roles in **IT, administration, logistics, or warehouse operations**, where structured processes and teamwork play a central role.
