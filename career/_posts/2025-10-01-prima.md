<section class="lead" style="text-align: justify;" markdown="1">
In October 2025, I joined <strong>Prima PMI</strong> in Melbourne as a <strong>Machine Operator (Christmas Casual)</strong>.  
During the peak production season, I worked across multiple departments—including **UV Lamination, Calender, Guillotine (Cut Finishing), and Shipping**—supporting high-volume manufacturing and fulfilment operations.

This role provided immersive experience in a full-scale industrial production environment, where I learned the importance of precision, adaptability, safety, and teamwork in keeping operations running efficiently under time pressure.
</section>

---

## ✨ UV Lamination Department

In this department, I was responsible for the protective and aesthetic coating of printed materials, ensuring every page met quality standards before moving to finishing.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Operated industrial UV lamination equipment to apply high-quality finishes to printed sheets.
- Monitored the lamination process to ensure every single page was correctly coated without bubbles, creases, or debris.
- Managed heat and speed settings to maintain consistency across large batches.
- Performed quality checks on adhesion and surface clarity.
- Troubleshooting feed issues to prevent material waste.

---

## 🏭 Calender Department

The Calender Department handles punching, drilling, tabbing, and finishing processes. I supported both mainline production and specialised finishing tasks using various industrial systems.

### High-volume punching & binding systems
**My involvement:**
- Assisted with continuous high-volume production runs on automated punching machinery.
- Material feeding, alignment checks, and output monitoring.
- Ensured punch consistency and workflow stability.
- Performed routine cleaning and basic maintenance during shift changes.

### Digital punching for customised requirements
**My involvement:**
- Assisted with setup for customised punching jobs on digital finishing equipment.
- Adjusted material positioning based on job specifications.
- Checked punch accuracy and hole alignment.

### Precision drilling for paper products
**My involvement:**
- Assisted with paper positioning and drilling accuracy using industrial paper drills.
- Monitored hole consistency across batches and helped with drill-bit checks.

---

## ✂️ Guillotine Department (Cut Finishing)

I supported cut finishing for paper-based products, ensuring raw prints were trimmed to exact final dimensions.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Assisting with heavy-duty industrial guillotine operations.
- Preparing material stacks for accurate cutting and checking measurements post-cut.
- Maintaining strict safety protocols around cutting equipment.

---

## 📦 Shipping Department

I supported the final stage of the production pipeline—fulfilment and dispatch.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Preparing finished products for shipment, ensuring correct labelling and packing.
- Verifying orders using **RF scanning systems**.
- Coordinating with team members to meet strict dispatch deadlines.

---

## 🔄 Understanding End-to-End Production Workflow

Working across **Lamination → Calender → Guillotine → Shipping** allowed me to understand the full production lifecycle.

**Key learnings:**
- **Quality at the Start:** Ensuring perfect lamination prevents expensive waste in the punching and cutting stages.
- **Communication:** Clear handovers between departments avoid bottlenecks.
- **Precision:** Small errors in the early stages (like a slight misalignment in lamination or punching) compound into significant issues at the shipping stage.

---

## 🔧 Maintenance & Machine Care

In addition to operation, I regularly supported basic maintenance tasks to minimize downtime:
- Cleaning sensitive machine components (rollers, sensors, and drill bits).
- Safely clearing jams in high-speed feeders.
- Identifying minor mechanical irregularities early to prevent equipment failure.
