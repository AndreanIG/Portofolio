## ✨ UV Lamination Department (Primary Role)

I currently work as the **primary and specialised operator for UV lamination systems**, a high-precision process requiring technical understanding of machine calibration, material behaviour, and quality control.

I am responsible for independently operating the UV lamination line and ensuring continuous, stable production output.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Operate industrial UV lamination equipment for protective and high-quality finishing of printed materials
- Sole operator responsible for UV lamination systems during production shifts
- Manage machine calibration including heat, speed, and feed adjustments based on material type
- Monitor lamination quality to ensure consistent coating without bubbles, creases, or contamination
- Perform real-time troubleshooting to prevent jams, feed errors, and production interruptions
- Conduct continuous quality inspection to ensure all output meets production standards
- Maintain production stability while managing high-volume workflow demands

This role requires strong technical control, attention to detail, and the ability to maintain precision under continuous production pressure.
