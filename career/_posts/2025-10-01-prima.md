In October 2025, I joined **Prima PMI** in Melbourne as a **Machine Operator (Christmas Casual)**, brought on to support the business through its busiest production period.

During the casual period, I rotated across the **Calender, Guillotine, and Shipping** departments, picking up the pace and standards of a high-volume manufacturing environment.

My consistency and attention to detail didn't go unnoticed. As the season progressed, I was promoted into a **dedicated UV Lamination & Foiling Specialist** role — becoming the **sole operator trusted to run the machine independently**, a responsibility usually reserved for far more experienced staff.

## 🌟 From Casual to Specialist

What started as a temporary Christmas casual position quickly turned into something more. After proving myself across multiple departments, management identified me as the right person to take full ownership of the UV lamination and foiling line — a role with real technical demands and zero room for error.

Being made the **sole operator** for this line meant the business was relying on me alone to keep this stage of production running smoothly, every shift, with no one else to fall back on.

---

## ✨ UV Lamination & Foiling Specialist (Current Role)

I am now the **sole specialist responsible for UV lamination and foiling**, a high-precision finishing process that requires deep technical understanding of machine calibration, material behaviour, and quality control.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Independently operate and manage the UV lamination and foiling line as the only trained specialist on shift
- Calibrate heat, speed, and feed settings to match different material types and job specifications
- Deliver consistent, defect-free finishing — free of bubbles, creases, lifting, or contamination
- Diagnose and resolve issues in real time to prevent jams, misfeeds, and downtime
- Carry out continuous quality inspection to ensure every job meets production standards
- Maintain output and stability under high-volume, deadline-driven conditions
- Act as the go-to person for this stage of production, with no secondary operator to rely on

This role demands technical precision, sound judgement, and the confidence to run a critical piece of equipment solo — day in, day out.

## 🏭 Where It Started: Casual Support Roles

Before stepping into the specialist role, I built a strong foundation across several departments during my casual period:

### Calender Department
- Assisted with automated punching and finishing machinery
- Supported material feeding, alignment, and output monitoring
- Performed basic machine cleaning and maintenance between shifts

### Guillotine Department (Cut Finishing)
- Assisted with industrial cutting operations for printed materials
- Prepared and aligned material stacks for accurate trimming
- Followed strict safety procedures during machine operation

### Shipping Department
- Prepared and packed finished goods for dispatch
- Verified orders using RF scanning systems
- Supported timely fulfilment of outgoing shipments
