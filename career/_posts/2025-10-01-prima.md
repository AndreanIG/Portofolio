<section class="lead" style="text-align: justify;" markdown="1">
In October 2025, I joined <strong>Prima PMI</strong> in Melbourne as a <strong>Machine Operator (Christmas Casual)</strong>.  
During the peak production season, I worked across <strong>three departments — Calender, Guillotine (Cut Finishing), and Shipping</strong>, supporting high-volume manufacturing and fulfilment operations.

This role was my first experience in a full-scale industrial production environment, where I learned the importance of precision, adaptability, safety, and teamwork in keeping operations running efficiently under time pressure.
</section>

---

## 🏭 Calender Department (Primary Role)

The Calender Department handles punching, drilling, tabbing, and finishing processes for a wide range of paper-based products.  
I spent most of my shifts here, supporting both mainline production and specialised finishing tasks.

---

- Ensured punch consistency and workflow stability  
- Performed routine cleaning and basic maintenance during shift changes  

This machine formed the backbone of daily calender production and required constant attention to maintain speed and quality.

## ✂️ Guillotine Department (Cut Finishing)

In the Guillotine Department, I supported cut finishing for paper-based products across various production jobs.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Assisting with guillotine cutting operations  
- Preparing stacks and materials for accurate cutting  
- Checking measurements and alignment before and after cuts  
- Handling finished products and preparing them for downstream processes  
- Maintaining cleanliness and safety around cutting equipment  

This role reinforced the importance of precision and safety, as small errors could impact large production batches.

---

## 📦 Shipping Department

In the Shipping Department, I supported the final stage of the production pipeline — fulfilment and dispatch.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Preparing finished products for shipment  
- Ensuring correct labelling, packing, and documentation  
- Verifying orders using RF scanning systems  
- Coordinating with team members to meet dispatch deadlines  
- Supporting loading and movement of outgoing goods  

This role gave me visibility into how production outputs connect directly to customer delivery.

---

## 🔄 Understanding End-to-End Production Workflow

Working across <strong>Calender → Guillotine → Shipping</strong> allowed me to understand the full production lifecycle.

Key learnings:
- Early-stage quality control prevents downstream rework  
- Clear communication between departments avoids bottlenecks  
- Timing and preparation are as important as speed  
- Small delays can impact the entire workflow  

This experience strengthened my operational awareness and process mindset.

---

## 🔧 Maintenance & Machine Care

In addition to machine operation, I regularly supported basic maintenance tasks across departments:

- Cleaning machine components and work areas  
- Clearing paper jams safely  
- Preparing machines for the next production run  
- Identifying minor issues early to minimise downtime  

This hands-on exposure helped me build strong safety awareness and respect for machine care.
